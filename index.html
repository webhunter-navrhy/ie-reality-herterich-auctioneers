Welcome to seaside living at its best. 100 m from the shore, this bright and spacious 2-bedroom first-floor apartment is perfectly positioned in the heart of Tramore`s vibrant beach community, offering breathtaking sea views and instant access to the beach and the town`s buzzing coastal lifestyle.

Just a short stroll from the promenade, surf schools, coffee spots, restaurants, and sandy shoreline, this apartment puts you right in the centre of it all. Whether you`re into sunrise swims, weekend markets, or sunset walks along the dunes, this is a location that truly delivers on lifestyle.

Inside, the apartment features a generous open-plan living area with a cosy solid fuel stove, laminated wooden flooring, and boasts a balcony overlooking the Atlantic Ocean and iconic Tramore sand hills.

The adjoining kitchen comes with tiled flooring and a practical layout, perfect for apartment living.

There are two spacious bedrooms, each with laminate wooden floors and fitted wardrobes. The master bedroom enjoys sea views and includes a fully tiled en-suite with WC, WHB, and a Triton electric shower. A second main bathroom offers a tiled floor, bath, WC, and WHB.

Adding to its appeal, this beautiful apartment is located within a well-maintained development with secure, gated access and private parking for residents.

Whether you`re looking for a permanent home or a weekend retreat, this apartment offers the rare combination of location, views, and lifestyle in one of Ireland`s most loved seaside towns.
